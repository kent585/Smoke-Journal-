# Thanksgiving practice turkey, 17 lb whole bird (2026-11-15)

- Smoker: recteq Flagship 2026. Pellets: mix of Competition Blend and Supreme Blend (Lumberjack).
- Prep: overnight brine, olive oil + Hey Grill Hey Sweet Rub, about a cup of butter under the breast skin. No apples or oranges in the cavity.
- Drippings pan on the grate with a can of chicken broth plus water (saved for gravy).
- Cook: 225 setting (ran about 235F) for most of the cook, bumped to 325-350F for roughly the last 45 min. Thermometer kept predicting done in 1, 2, 2.5 hrs, which was wrong.
- Result: about 5 hrs, ~17-18 min/lb. Plateau 135-150F. Pulled breast 166F, peaked 169F.
- Rating: 5 stars. Very moist, lots of flavor, family tasted the smoke. Nobody eats the skin.
- Lessons: butter plus brine works. Don't bump heat early, hold in a cooler instead. If behind, 275F not 325F. 17 lb was the biggest that's worked; earlier 19-20 lb birds came out dry.
