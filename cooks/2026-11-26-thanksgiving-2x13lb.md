# Thanksgiving, two 13 lb turkeys (2026-11-26) - DONE

- Birds: 13.41 lb and 12.79 lb (store labels, $0.99/lb, use-by 11/30 and 11/27).
- Photos: photos/2026-11-26-thanksgiving/

- Rating: 5 stars. "Best turkey" yet, so many compliments.
- Smoker: recteq Flagship 2026 at 225 setting. Brine in the fridge ~6 AM Wed 11/25, out ~8:30 PM (about 14.5 hrs), air-dried uncovered overnight.
- Probes (Chef iQ, in the breasts) started 7:40 AM at about 35F. Raw data in data/.
- Breast timeline (probe 1 / probe 2): 100F at 9:29 / 9:26, 130F at 10:39 / 10:42, 150F at 12:03 / 12:27, 160F at 13:28 / 13:58, 165F at 14:02 / 14:15. Peak 168.6F / 167.9F.
- Grill ambient averaged about 230 to 260F depending on the probe (probe 1 side ran about 25F hotter). Heat was raised around 1:30 PM and ambient hit about 290 to 330F for the last stretch.
- Total about 6.6 hrs on the grill, then a 25 min rest. The plan said 4 to 4.5 hrs, so the plan was wrong by about 2 hrs. Two 13 lb birds ran about 30 min/lb versus about 18 min/lb for the single 17 lb bird.
- Not logged yet: pellets, rub, what time dinner was served.

## Lessons
- With two birds in at once, plan about 6.5 hrs from a cold start (30 min/lb), not 4.5.
- The 150 to 160F stretch took 1.4 to 1.5 hrs. That's the plateau, not a problem.
- Still moist and flavorful with a brine, butter under the skin, and a pull at 165F.
