Turkey Day Cook Plan

  1. 10:00 AM

    Before church

    Take the turkey out of the brine. Pat it really dry, inside and out. If it was a store-bought poultry brine, skip the rinse. If it was a homemade salt brine, a quick rinse is fine.

    Set it on a rack or sheet pan, uncovered, and put it back in the fridge. The air dry helps the skin crisp and keeps the bird cold while you're gone.

    Fridge, uncovered
  2. 11:30 AM

    Home from church, fire it up

    Preheat the recteq to 325°F. Rub the turkey with oil or butter and your seasoning. Get a probe in the thickest part of the breast and another in the thigh, away from the bone.

    Preheat 325°F
  3. 12:00 PM

    Turkey goes on

    Breast side up, directly on the grates. Keep the lid closed. Every peek can cost you up to 20% of your heat.

    Expect roughly 13 minutes a pound, so about 3.5 to 3.75 hours for a 17 lb bird.

    325–350°F, full smoke
  4. 3:00 PM

    Start checking temps

    Probe both the breast and the thigh. If the skin is getting dark before the meat is ready, tent the breast loosely with foil.

  5. 3:30–4:00

    Pull it

    Pull when the breast reads 160°F and the thigh reads 175°F. Don't wait for 165 in the breast, because carryover will take it there while it rests.

    Breast 160°F · Thigh 175°F
  6. 4:30 PM

    Rest

    Tent loosely with foil for 30 minutes. If it finished early, wrap it in foil and towels and hold it in a dry cooler. It stays hot for a couple of hours.

    30 min rest
  7. 5:00 PM

    Carve and eat

    Save the drippings for gravy.

If it runs fast or slow

  • Running early: wrap it in foil and towels, then hold it in a dry cooler until 4:30.
  • Running late: bump the grill to 350°F. Keep the lid closed and trust the probes.
  • Skin dark, meat not done: tent the breast with foil and keep cooking.

Times are estimates. Go by the probe, not the clock. Sources: recteq Turkey 101, Hey Grill Hey, Traeger, Weber, ThermoWorks.
