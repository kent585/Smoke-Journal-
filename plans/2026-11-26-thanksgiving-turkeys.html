Thanksgiving Turkey Plan

Two 13 lb turkeys on the recteq. Brined, air-dried, smoked at 225. Dinner at 2:00.

  1. Wed 8:30 PM

    Out of the brine

    Brine went in the fridge around 6 AM Wednesday, so pull them between 8 and 10 PM. 8:30 PM is the sweet spot (about 14.5 hours). Don't go past 10 PM. Pat really dry, set each on a rack in a pan, and put them in the fridge uncovered on the bottom shelf. Keep the fridge at or below 40°F.

    Out of brine ~8:30 PM
  2. 7:45 AM

    Out of the fridge

    Push about a cup of butter under the breast skin on each bird. Olive oil and rub on top, lighter on the breast so the crust doesn't scorch. Probe in each breast. Set up the drippings pans with a can of broth plus water.

  3. 8:00 AM

    Fire up

    Preheat the recteq to 225°F with a full smoke.

    Preheat 225°F
  4. 8:30 AM

    Both birds on

    Side by side with a little space between them for airflow. Keep the lid closed. Two birds together run slower than one, so expect 4 to 4.5 hours.

    225°F, full smoke
  5. 11:30 AM

    Pace check

    Breasts should be around 130–135°F. If you're behind, raise the grill to 275°F, not 325. The sugar in the rub burned last time.

  6. 12:30–1:15

    Pull

    Pull when the breast reads 160–165°F. Don't trust the thermometer's time estimates, go by the temp.

    Breast 160–165°F · Thigh 175°F
  7. Hold

    Keep them hot until carving

    Wrap in foil and towels and put them in a dry cooler. They stay hot for a couple of hours, so finishing early is fine.

  8. 1:45 PM

    Carve, eat at 2:00

    Save the drippings for gravy.

Lessons from last cook

  • A cup of butter under the skin plus the brine gave a very moist breast.
  • At a 225 setting, your grill ran about 235°F and a 17 lb bird took about 5 hours, around 18 minutes a pound.
  • Don't bump the heat out of fear it'll finish early. Hold it in a cooler instead.
